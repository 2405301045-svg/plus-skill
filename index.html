The College-to-Career Gap

The hard truth facing college graduates in India

Degrees check an application box. But when hiring managers open resumes, they don't look for college GPA — they search for proven output.

56.35% 2026 Milestone

Graduate Employability

Barely 56.35% of Indian higher-education graduates meet real-world job readiness standards. More than 43% require extensive retraining before contributing to production environments.

Student Reality:

Graduating with high marks without verified code or project artifacts leaves students in hyper-competitive generic queues.

72% Curriculum Lag

Curriculum Misalignment

72% of college syllabi are misaligned with modern corporate engineering and growth workflows. Syllabus cycles take 4-6 years to update while technology stacks evolve quarterly.

Student Reality:

Exam papers ask for pen-and-paper code while the industry expects Git PRs, CI/CD, microservice hygiene, and API telemetry.

64% Recruiter Shift

Employers Favour Practical Skills

64% of employers and tech hiring leads now prioritize verified practical skills, live project execution, and validated portfolios over college brand pedigree or academic tier.

Student Reality:

A BCA student with verified project proof consistently beats a tier-1 graduate who has only theoretical certificates.

Source note: India Skills Report 2026

Key findings on graduate employability indices, curriculum alignment benchmarks, and recruiter hiring preferences across Tier 1, 2, and 3 Indian educational institutions.
